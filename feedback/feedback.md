# User Feedback — Phyat Paing

- **How collected:** In-person demo + follow-up message (2 people)
- **When:** 2026-07-24
- **Who:** Friend (college student) + Family member (shop owner)

## Raw feedback

1. "The upload works great but I didn't know I could drag & drop — I kept clicking the button. Maybe add a hint text."
2. "I want to see my total spending for this month right when I open the app, without clicking around."
3. "The dark mode is nice but the text in charts is hard to read in dark mode — too low contrast."
4. "Can I split a bill? Like if me and my roommate share electricity, I want to track my half."
5. "Adding tags or notes to bills would be useful — like 'paid late' or 'shared with Aung'."
6. "The mobile view works but the upload page feels cramped on my phone screen."
7. "My internet bill is the same every month — can it auto-fill when I select 'recurring'?"
8. "What happens if I upload a non-bill photo? It accepted a random receipt from a tea shop."
9. "The CSV export is helpful but I want to see the original currency amounts too, not just MMK."
10. "Login page looks clean but there's no 'forgot password' option."

## Themes (what keeps coming up)

- **UX polish** — drag & drop hint, dark mode chart contrast, mobile spacing
- **Missing features** — forgot password, bill splitting, tags/notes, auto-fill recurring
- **Data visibility** — monthly total on dashboard, original currency in export
- **Edge cases** — non-bill uploads accepted, no recurring auto-fill

## Top 3 things to fix

- [ ] Add drag & drop visual hint text below the upload area
- [ ] Fix dark mode chart text contrast (too low on dark background)
- [ ] Add monthly total spending summary to the dashboard hero area
